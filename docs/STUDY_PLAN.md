# Proposed study · Estudo proposto

## First 90 days after receiving a unit / Primeiros 90 dias após receber a unidade

| Period / Período | Work / Trabalho | Evidence / Evidência |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–15 | Confirm edition, firmware, permitted native features and storage behavior with Vbot; inspect unit and accessories. / Confirmar versão, funções e armazenamento. | Capability inventory and operator checklist. / Inventário e lista de verificação. |
| Days 16–30 | Rehearse synthetic scenarios with operator observations in an empty indoor course. / Ensaiar cenários em percurso interno vazio. | Measured parameters, reproducible reports and stated limitations. / Parâmetros medidos e relatos reproduzíveis. |
| Days 31–60 | Repeat permitted tasks and document failures, operator workload and any localization gaps. / Repetir tarefas permitidas e documentar falhas. | Aggregate engineering findings; no unconsented imagery. / Resultados agregados. |
| Days 61–90 | Publish an initial engineering report and bilingual teaching materials; discuss next steps with the manufacturer. / Publicar relatório inicial e material bilíngue. | Public code, methods, findings and sponsor acknowledgment if applicable. / Código, métodos e reconhecimento do apoio quando houver. |

## 12-month direction / Direção em 12 meses

Subject to feasibility and necessary permissions, develop educational workshops, community demonstrations, replication guides and a candid Brazilian case study. No audience size, sales conversion, participant benefit, university endorsement or publication acceptance is guaranteed.

The program can begin with a standard Vbot and native controls. Open SDK access is not required. Any later adapter would be optional and permitted by the manufacturer.
